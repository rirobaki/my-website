About Us

Math is for everyone who dares to think

Mu Alpha Theta (μαθ) is the national mathematics honor society for high school students — dedicated to inspiring mathematical curiosity, developing strong scholarship, and building a community around the love of math.

The Cinco Ranch chapter competes at local, state, and national levels, runs a well-attended peer tutoring program, and hosts events that bring the entire school community together around mathematics.

Whether you're chasing a perfect AMC score, struggling through Precalculus, or just want to find your people — this is your chapter.

Academic Excellence

Membership recognizes students with outstanding mathematical achievement and a commitment to scholarship.

Peer Tutoring

Members volunteer their time helping fellow Cougars from Algebra I through AP Calculus BC — at no cost.

Competition Team

We compete in AMC 10/12, TMSCA, the University of Houston competition, and more throughout the school year.

Weekly Meetings

We meet every week in Mrs. Fosli's room — for tutoring, competition prep, and chapter business.

Tutoring Program

Free help, when you need it

Struggling before a test? Come by every Wednesday — no sign-up, no requirements, no strings. Our peer tutors are fellow students who know the material and know how to explain it.

Compete

Math on the big stage

National · November
AMC 10 / AMC 12

The MAA's flagship competition — 30 multiple-choice questions, 75 minutes. We host both exams at CRHS every November, open to all members.

State · Full Year
TMSCA

Texas Math and Science Coaches Association. We compete in number sense, calculator applications, and general math at invitational and district meets.

Local · University of Houston
UoH Competition

An invitational hosted by the University of Houston — a great opportunity to compete on a college campus against top Houston-area math students.
